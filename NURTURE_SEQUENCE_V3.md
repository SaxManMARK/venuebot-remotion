# VenueBot Nurture Sequence V3 — Day-by-Day Summary

A complete map of the 26-touchpoint AI nurturing sequence for VenueBot Studio, organised into 6 stages with conditional branching depending on whether the couple books a tour.

---

## Stage 1 — Enquiry Response (immediate)

| When | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| T+0 min | Email (E1.1) | Auto-acknowledge + brochure delivery |
| T+5 min | SMS (E1.2) | Warm human confirmation from coordinator |
| T+2 hours / next business morning | Email (E1.3) | Personalised welcome — orient to date, guests, fit |

---

## Stage 2 — Pre-Tour Nurture (if no tour booked)

| Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Day 3 | Email (E2.1) | Real wedding + matched testimonial (reassure) |
| Day 5 | Email (E2.2) | Specific venue feature / experience highlight |
| Day 7 | SMS (E2.3) | Short availability nudge + booking link |
| Day 9 | Email (E2.4) | Live video walk-and-talk offer |
| Day 12 | WhatsApp (E2.5) | Personal voice note / conversational rescue |
| Day 14 | Email (E2.6) | Respectful close-loop, door left open |

---

## Stage 3 — Tour Confirmation & Reminders (if tour booked)

| When | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| On booking | Email (TC1) | Full confirmation, logistics, what to expect |
| T-3 days | SMS (TC2) | Reminder + "anything to cover?" |
| T-1 day | WhatsApp (TC3) | Voice note / warm personal message |
| Morning of tour | SMS (TC4) | Directions + direct contact number |

---

## Stage 4 — Post-Tour Follow-Up

| Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Same day | Email (PT1) | Personalised thank-you referencing the visit |
| Same day (+ few hours) | SMS (PT-SMS) | Short warm follow-up |
| Day 2 | Email (PT2) | Package clarity, inclusions, next steps |
| Day 4 | Email (PT3) | Booking ask with real date urgency |
| Day 7 | SMS / WhatsApp (PT4) | "Happy to answer any final questions" |
| Day 10 | Email (PT5) | Respectful soft close-loop |

## Stage 5 — Pre-Tour Recovery (no tour after Day 14)

| Day | Channel | Purpose |

## Stage 6 — Post-Tour Recovery (toured but no booking after Day 10)

| When | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|

| Post-tour Day 35 | Email (PTR4) | Respectful farewell, door left open |

---

## Channel Mix

Across the full architecture:

| Channel | Messages | % of Total | Primary Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email | 19 | 65% | Content depth, social proof, orientation, booking asks, recovery |
| SMS | 8 | 28% | Immediacy, reminders, short nudges, recovery |
| WhatsApp | 2 | 7% | Relationship-building, personal voice notes at high-leverage moments |

**Design notes:**
- SMS is reserved for immediacy and brevity — never content-heavy delivery
- WhatsApp is used for the two highest-relationship moments: day-before tour and Day 12 rescue
- Email carries all substantive value: social proof, package detail, FAQ, booking asks
- No channel duplicates another's content at any stage
