## Product

**VenueBot** is a complete **enquiry-to-booking system built specifically for wedding venues.** It is delivered as **SWAS — Software With A Service** (never call it "SaaS"). It is **not** a chatbot and **not** just a CRM. Never reference the underlying white-labelled platform.

Organised around three connected pillars:
- **Studio AI** — *Generate* more enquiries (Knowledge): understands what makes a venue unique, identifies opportunities, benchmarks competitors, improves booking-influencing content.
- **Convert** — *Convert* more couples (Engagement): responds instantly, nurtures automatically, answers day and night, ensures no opportunity goes cold.
- **Care** — *Manage* everything in one place (Control): conversations, bookings, marketing, reporting, automation, and AI assistants for the whole venue journey.

Outcome: **More bookings. More confidence.**

## Ideal customer (ICP)

Wedding **venue owners, general managers, and wedding coordinators** making a significant commercial decision. Time-poor, proud of their venue, emotionally invested, not technologists. They respond to relief, control, confidence, and evidence — not jargon or hype.

## Core positioning & differentiation

**Most venues don't have an enquiry problem. They have a conversion problem.** Bookings are won or lost in the moments *after* a couple gets in touch — the response, the follow-up, the answered question. Competitors are point tools, chatbots, or generic CRMs; VenueBot is the single wedding-specific system that closes the gap end to end. We sell **relief**, not software. Make owners feel recognised, not criticised.

## Proof points

- 81% of couples say they've felt frustrated by venue response times.
- Nearly 70% make their decision after visiting three venues or fewer.
- Almost 70% of wedding enquiries happen outside normal office hours.
